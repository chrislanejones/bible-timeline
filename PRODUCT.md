# Bible Timeline

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Readers studying biblical history use a shared timeline to connect stories, rulers, prophets, and Scripture. It must support exploration on a phone as well as comparison on a desktop, without requiring an account or network connection for the timeline itself.

## Confirmed direction
The user approved the seven improvements from the app review: reliable event selection, nearby mobile search and reading, bounded desktop layout, a simpler overview and chronological list, readable labels and keyboard focus, contemporary relationships, and shareable views with bookmarks. They also requested additional sourced events and a README.

A later request extended the atlas back to Genesis and up to the eve of Matthew, asked for a good/bad toggle for kings, removed the sidebar story list so the timeline could expand, and asked for a card that hovers over the timeline for the selected entry.

## Design principles
Preserve the existing warm dark palette, serif headings, and restrained historical character. Prioritize reading and navigation over decoration. Reveal detail as readers zoom. Explain approximate dates and avoid implying that contemporaries necessarily met. Never assign a year to stories Scripture leaves undated; show them as undated. Say plainly which chronology the early dates follow and that it is debated. Keep the standalone, dependency-free app.

## Accessibility
Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable type, touch targets, reduced motion, and textual equivalents to color. Mobile reading must remain in view and return focus to its trigger.
