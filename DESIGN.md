# Design

Warm charcoal surfaces, cream text, gold event accents, blue/red king ratings, green prophets, and purple later history come from tokens.css. Georgia headings and system sans-serif text preserve the existing visual identity.

The desktop gives the timeline the full width. The selected entry opens in a floating card placed beside its mark, on the roomier side, inside the visible chart area; a mark too wide for either side gets the card below it. The card can be dragged or hidden, and steps aside for a button when its entry leaves the view. A hover preview names a mark without selecting it. The range navigator stays pinned to the bottom of wide screens. Narrow screens, the story list, and the saved view use a native reading dialog instead of the card.

Undated stories use a dashed circle at the start of the axis. In the broad overview the three early lanes share one “Before the kings” band, and phones group its people the way reigns are grouped. Search results remain adjacent to search. Shared controls use descriptive labels and visible focus. Era navigation and clustered marks disclose detail without overcrowding the overview.

This is a usability refinement of the existing app. Image probes and new visual mockups are unnecessary: retain the reviewed visual direction and verify the interaction changes in the browser.
