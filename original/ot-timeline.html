A note on dates & king ratings

Dates are approximate BC dates, following a conventional chronology of the monarchy. Early stories are positioned illustratively; co-regencies are simplified. Ezra’s return is shown at the traditional date of 458 BC, and Nehemiah’s at 445 BC. Joel and Obadiah have disputed dates. Prophet tracks indicate approximate activity, not lifespans. “Major” and “minor” refer to book length, not importance. King colors summarize the biblical assessment, with mixed reigns and repentance explained in the notes. A faithful rating does not imply a flawless life.
